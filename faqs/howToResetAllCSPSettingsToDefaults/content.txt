# How to reset all CSP settings to defaults

---

# Want to reset every CSP setting? Follow this guide!

---

# Method 1
> 1. Close Content Manager
> 2. Open File Explorer and go to **Documents/AssettoCorsa**
> 3. Rename the `cfg` folder to `cfg_old`
> 
> *This will **wipe all** of your presets, but you can always rename cfg_old back to cfg if it does not fix your issue.*
[attachment: ResetCSPExample.png]

---

## 🚨  Please ensure you change the cfg folder that is in the Documents folder and NOT in the root folder 🚨
