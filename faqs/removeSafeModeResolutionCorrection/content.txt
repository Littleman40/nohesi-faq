# Remove SafeMode Resolution Correction

---

# Here is how to fix “Running in SafeMode Resolution Correction”
[attachment: RemoveSafemodeLOGO.png]

---

## Step 1
> Open Content Manager and go to Settings > Assetto Corsa > Video (in Content Manager)
[attachment: Remove_SafeMode_Resolution_Correction.png]

---

## Step 2
> Make sure your resolution settings match your monitors settings, for example if you have 1080p, select 1920x1080 @ whichever hz your screen runs at.
