# How to Setup Teleport to pits

---

# Wondering how to quickly teleport back to pits when you crash? Follow this guide!
[attachment: How_to_Setup_Teleport_to_pitsLOGO.png]

---

> Open Content Manager and and go to Settings > Assetto Corsa > Controls > System
> Then bind "**Teleport to pits**" and/or "**Start/Setup to pits**"
[attachment: How_to_Setup_Teleport_to_pits.png]
