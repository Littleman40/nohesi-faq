# How to fix Lost ForceFeedBack (FFB) when Alt-Tabbing

---

# Playing No Hesi then when you Alt-Tab you lose all Force Feedback? Follow this guide!
[attachment: How_to_fix_lost_FFBLOGO.png]

---

# Microsoft pushed out "Microsoft GameInput" Redistributable as part of one of the Xbox App updates (I think?) and that has been claiming exclusivity over FFB devices and causing issues.

# Method 1
-# Do these steps to disable the service. Simply uninstalling isn't permanent as Windows seems to reinstall it on reboot:
## Step 1
> Press Windows Key and type "Services" to find the Service Manager. Open it as administrator.
[attachment: How_to_fix_Lost_ForceFeedBack_FFB_when_Alt-Tabbing1.png]

---

## Step 2
> Find "GameInput Service" and right click > Properties > Startup Type > Change to "Disabled" > Click OK
[attachment: How_to_fix_Lost_ForceFeedBack_FFB_when_Alt-Tabbing2.png]
