# System.IO.IOException: The cloud file provider is not running

---

# Here is how to fix "System.IO.IOException: The cloud file provider is not running".
[attachment: image.png]

---

## Step 1
> On the taskbar on the bottom of your screen, click on the OneDrive icon 
## Step 2
> Navigate to Settings > Sync and Backup 
## Step 3
> Click on the Download files button.
