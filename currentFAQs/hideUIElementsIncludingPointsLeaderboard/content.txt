# Hide UI elements (including Points) Leaderboard

---

# Pit Menu (on the right):
> Settings > Assetto Corsa > System > **"Pits menu" disable everything**
> Settings > Custom Shader Patch > General Patch Settings > Enable **"Stop AC from using D Pad in pits app"**
> Settings > Assetto Corsa > Controls > "System" > Enable **'Ignore Pov1 buttons in pits"**

# or
** **
> Open Steam > Right click Assetto Corsa > click properties > click local files > click browse
> Go to \system\cfg\pitstop.ini
> Open the .ini file with notepad and then change USE_MOUSE_PITSTOP=0 to be **=1**
> Relaunch Content Manager
[attachment: HideUILOGO.png]

---

# Driver names (bottom):
> **Method 1:**
> Press F9 until the bar is completely hidden
> 
> **Method 2:**
> Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Assetto Corsa -> View & UI -> Uncheck: `Show leaderboard`
[attachment: HideUI1.png]

# Virtual Mirror (top mid) :
> **Method 1:**
> Press F11
> 
> **Method 2:**
> Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Assetto Corsa -> View & UI -> Uncheck: `Show virtual mirror`
[attachment: HideUI2.png]

# Proximity indicator (red arrow):
> Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Assetto Corsa -> System -> Proximity indicator -> Uncheck: `Show nearest opponents`

# Server messages (top mid)
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Assetto Corsa -> View & UI -> Messages
> 2. Uncheck whichever messages you do not wish to see
[attachment: HideUI5.png]

---

# Ping (top right):
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> GUI
> 2. Check: `Hide ping text in the top right corner`
[attachment: HideUI6.png]

---

# Time Remaining in Session (Top Middle)
> 1. Open <#1322235675239645184> -> `system` -> `cfg` -> `session_info.ini`
> 2. Modify/ set the following values:
> ```"PRATICE_VISIBLE=0"
> "QUALIFY_VISIBLE=0"
> "RACE_VISIBLE=0"```
[attachment: HideUI7.png]

---

# Pit speed limit (top left)
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> GUI -> Extra HUD Elements
> 2. Uncheck all the boxes
[attachment: HideUI9.png]

---

# No Hesi Points UI / Leaderboard UI
> Click the `action bar` located at the top right of your screen.
> 
> - To turn off all of the Points UI, click the `toggle button` in the `Toggle UI` box
>      - To turn off specific Points UI parts, click `UI Settings` at the bottom, then click the `toggle button` for the specific part you want disabled.
[attachment: HideUI10.png]

---

# No Hesi Speedometer / Chat
> Click the `action bar` located at the top right of your screen.
> 
> To turn off the speedometer, click the `toggle button` in the `Speedometer UI`
> 
> To turn off chat, click the `toggle button` in the `Chat UI`
[attachment: HideUI11.png]

---


# Red Crash Flash Effect
> To remove the red crash effect that shows around the border of the screen:
> 
> Click the `action bar` in the top right, click `UI settings`, and uncheck `Crash Flash Effect`
[attachment: HideUI12.png]

---

# Popup Score Notifications
> To hide these popup score notifications in the bottom right of your screen:
> 
> Click the `action bar` in the top right, click `UI settings`, and uncheck `Score Notifications`
[attachment: HideUI13.png]
