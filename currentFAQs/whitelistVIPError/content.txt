# Step 1
**Whitelist your Steam & Discord**
> 1. Head over to [**our website**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile>)
> 2. Log in to your account
> 3. Click your profile picture -> edit profile -> social links
> 4. Link **both discord + steam**
> 
> **Note**:
> This can take 5-10 minutes to apply, and we suggest restarting your PC during this time.
[attachment: 1.png]

---

# Step 2 - If the above did not fix it
> Find your Steam ID, through the steam app -> profile picture -> account details.
[attachment: 2.png]

---

> Make sure the same steam ID is selected in Content Manager. 
> 
> CM -> Settings -> Content Manager -> General.
[attachment: 3.png]

---

> Make sure the same steam ID is also linked on the website, by heading to [**our website**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile>) -> profile picture -> edit profile -> social links
