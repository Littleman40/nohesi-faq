# If you are having the issue with cars:

## **Prerequisite**:
> **You must be on 0.2.11 CSP or newer**.
> -# Content Manager -> Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> About & Updates
## Step 1:
> 1. Go to Content Manager → Settings → Custom Shaders Patch → General Patch Settings → GPU Optimizations
> 2. Ensure you have "Optimize meshes some more" **DISABLED**.
> -# *Please note: this setting was removed in newer CSP 3.0 versions.*
[attachment: 1.png]

---

## Step 2: - If the above did NOT work:
> **Delete & reinstall the specific car's having issues**, following: <#1440122305861652520>

# If you are having the issue on the map 110:
## Step 1:
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> About & Updates
> 2. Install **0.2.11 CSP**
[attachment: 2.png]

---

## Step 3
**Download and Install 7zip. (*do NOT use WinRAR*)**
> 1. Open https://www.7-zip.org/
> 2. Click "Download"
> 3. Double-click the downloaded exe file
> 4. Follow the prompts to complete the install

---

## Step 4
**Delete the track:**
> 1. <#1322235675239645184> > content > tracks
> 2. Delete the 110 folder

---

## Step 5
**Reinstall the track, ensuring you install the `autoupateapp` lua folder.**
> 1. Download the correct 110 version:
>    * 110 V1 Public: [**download here**](<https://bcdn.nohesi.gg/maps/nohesi_110_public.7z>)
>    * 110 V1 Tier 3: [**download here**](<https://cdn.nohesi.gg/maps/nohesi_110.7z>) 
>    * 110 V2 Tier 3: [**download here**](<https://cdn.nohesi.gg/maps/nohesi_110_v2.7z>)
> 2. Right-Click the zip folder > Show more options > 7zip > Open archive
> 3. Drag & Drop the `apps` and `content` into <#1322235675239645184>
> -# This "apps" folder, contains the "autoupateapp" lua needed

---

## Step 6
> Ensure your **antivirus **/ **firewall **/ **ISP** are not blocking the connection to the server.
> 
> 1. **Antiviruses** -> Disable these or allow exceptions for Assetto Corsa and Content Manager
> 2. **Firewalls **-> Allow exceptions for Assetto Corsa and Content Manager
> 3. **ISPs** -> If this applies to you, most common for **Russian Users**, you will be required to use a **VPN** or **Proxy **to bypass this.
