# Questioning how to link everything together regarding No Hesi, Content Manager & Discord? Follow this guide!

---

# How To Link Steam To No Hesi / Whitelist Verify
> **Link both your Steam & Discord to your No Hesi website account** under the [**edit profile**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile>) tab.
> 
> **Note:**
> This can take 5-10 minutes to apply, and we suggest restarting your PC during this time.
[attachment: 1.png]

---

# How to link Steam to Content Manager
> Find your Steam ID through the Steam app -> profile picture -> account details.
> 
> Enter the same Steam ID in Content Manager through: CM -> Settings -> Content Manager -> General.
[attachment: 2.png]

---

# How to link Steam to Discord
> Press on the User Settings next to your username > Connections > Click Steam Icon and follow linking process
