## What this guide *CAN* and *CANNOT* do
> This guide will help you understand and optimise your settings for the best balance between quality and performance. Increase and decrease the settings as needed.
> 
> This guide WILL not magically increase your FPS to 300 at 4k resolution and it will not make your PC itself better if the specs are terrible.
> **This guide is meant for Custom Shaders Patch versions 0.2.x**

## What are some common causes of FPS issues?
- `Installing Pure by dragging it into CM.`
> This causes heavy FPS drops. Follow the video here to install it correctly: https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1322227974371545159

- `Your CPU/GPU is not strong enough for your monitor resolution.`
> If you have low-end hardware, avoid resolutions above 1080p. If you use a higher resolution monitor, lower the resolution in your GPU control panel.

- `Shadow and/or reflection resolution are set too high.`
> Values between 3K–8K are usually overkill. For low- to mid-tier PCs, keep this at 1024–2048.

- `MSAA is set to 8x.`
> Use 2x/turn it off on low-end systems, and 4x max if your PC can handle it.

- `Rain.`
> Avoid using this if you’re in need of more frames.

- `Tree skins.`
> While they can drastically change the look of maps, its not recommended to use them if you are using low to mid-tier hardware.

# __Assetto Corsa Video Settings__
## Resolution and FPS
- `Rendering Mode:`
> Set to match your screen set up i.e One Monitor = Single Screen.
> Tick fullscreen as windowed mode usually diminishes frames.

- `Resolution:`
> Set this to match your monitor's resolution and refresh rate.
> Please note that the higher your resolution, the more demanding it is on your FPS.
[attachment: VideoSettings1.png]

---

> Do not enable `Fix Resolution Automatically`, `Vertical Synchronization`, or `Limit framerate`.
[attachment: VideoSettings2.png]

---

## Quality:
- `MSAA:`
> Set to `2x` or `4x` depending on PC specs | Do not use `8x`.

- `Anisotropic Filtering:`
> Feel free to max this out.

- `World Details:`
> This is reliant on the track you are using but usually set this to `Medium` or `High`.

- `Shadow Resolution:`
> This is the biggest frame killer, set this to `512x512` or `1024x1024` | **NEVER** set this to anything above `2048x2048`.

- `Smoke Generation:`
> This setting is overridden by ParticlesFX but you can set this to `Medium` anyways.
[attachment: VideoSettings3.png]

---

## Reflections:
- `Reflection Resolution:`
> Set this to either `512x512` or `1024x1024`.

- `Reflection Frequency:`
> **ONLY** set this to `Two face per frame`.

- `Rendering Distance:`
> Feel free to max this out.
[attachment: VideoSettings4.png]

---

## Post-Processing
- `Enable Post-Processing Effects`
> Have this checked/enabled.

- `Filter:`
> Depending on the filter you use, the impact can vary but usually the `defaults` and the `pure_` (if pure is installed) ones are the best for FPS.

- `Overall Quality:`
> Setting this to `High` or `Very High` should be sufficient.

- `Glare Quality:`
> Setting this to `High` or `Very High` should be sufficient.

- `Depth of Field:`
> Setting this to `Maximum` is fine as it only works during replays.

- `Motion Blur:`
> Set this to `Off`

- `Saturation:`
> Leave this at 100%

- `Heat Shimmering`
> You can leave this off as it is not noticeable and can take some frames

- `Sunrays`
> Leave this on

- `FXAA`
> Leave this on
[attachment: VideoSettings5.png]

---

## Mirrors
- `Mirror Resolution:`
> `128x512` or `256x1024` is sufficient and should provide a good balance.

- `High Quality`
> Leave this on
[attachment: VideoSettings6.png]

# Custom Shaders Patch Settings
- `CSP Settings > DXGI`
> Make sure you have “Use new dxgi flip model” & “Triple buffering” checked, set Maximum latency to 1 frame.
[attachment: CSPSettings1.png]

---

- `General Patch Settings > GPU & CPU optimizations`
> > Make sure you have “Flatten nodes” & "Apply hyperthreading fix" checked, Set Chunks optimization to “advanced (experimental)”. Under GPU optimization enable "Optimize meshes" & "Optimize meshes some more"
> > **Important!**
> > Since the Chinatown addition to 110 it is recommended to disable “Optimize meshes some more”. 
> > To prevent any issues on the map keep this setting OFF! Set Chunk optimization to "Basic". If you don't have access to the 110 map / don’t play it you can ignore this.
[attachment: CSPSettings2.png]
