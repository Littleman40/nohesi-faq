# Handshake Failed

---

# Here is how to fix *Handshake Failed*
[attachment: Handshake_FailedLOGO.png]

---

# The most likely cause of this error is that someone took your car as you were loading in. Hit the refresh button next to join and use any car highlighted white.

---

# Prerequisite:
> This FAQ covers many steps to address all possible issues that can cause this error. If you are **crashing on the loading screen**, **take a look at the text in the bottom left, right before this error shows**. This text may say the root cause of your issue, avoiding you from having to go through all 12 steps.

---

>  - F2P → <#1147102029987196978>

# Method 3
> 1. Ensure you have set a **Name **and **Online Name**, **that isnt: `player`** and that consists of **only letters/numbers, NO SYMBOLS**

# Method 4

# Method 5
> Make sure **Content manager** and **Custom Shaders Patch** are up to date.
> 
> **Content Manager Reinstall:**
> https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1322240298210033804
> 
> **Custom Shaders Patch:**
> Content Manager -> Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> About & Updates -> Install 0.2.11 CSP or newer

# Method 6

# Method 9

> Make sure your computer clock settings are **correctly set, and synced.**

> Right-click the system clock in your **System Tray** at the bottom-right corner of the screen and click  **Adjust date/time** *(You can also go to Settings > Time & Language > Date & time.)*
### Ensure both `set timezone automatically` and `set time automatically` are ticked -> then press `sync now`.

---

-# **If any of these options are greyed out, please watch <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPq2R-vxCTw> first.**

### A full explanation + other debugging steps can be found on the article: <https://www.makeuseof.com/tag/3-reasons-windows-computer-loses-time-date/>
