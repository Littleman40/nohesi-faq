# How to get Pure (PAID MOD)

---

# Follow this guide to get pure and get those beautiful skydomes and more updated visuals. (**Does not include rain**)
# ⚠️ Paid $$$ mod ⚠️
[attachment: 3.png]

---

# Step 1
> Go to [**Pure's Patreon**](<https://www.patreon.com/c/peterboese/home>) and subscribe to the **lowest level** to gain access.

---

# Step 2
> 1. Click on the [**Posts Tab**](<https://www.patreon.com/c/peterboese/posts>) 
> 2. Find the latest release of pure
> 3. Download it

---

# Step 3
**Download and Install 7zip. (*do NOT use WinRAR*)**
> 1. Open https://www.7-zip.org/
> 2. Click "Download"
> 3. Double-click the downloaded exe file
> 4. Follow the prompts to complete the install
[attachment: 1.png]

---

# Step 4
**Install Pure manually:**
> 1. Locate the Pure ZIP file in your downloads folder
> 2. Right-Click -> (*Show More Options)* -> 7zip -> Open Archive
> 3. Inside of the 7zip window, click into the Pure folder
> 4. Drag & drop `apps`, `content`, `extension`, `system` into your AC root folder: <#1322235675239645184>
[attachment: 2.png]


---

# Step 5
> 1. Open Content Manager
> 2. Click Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> WeatherFX -> Weather Style
> 3. Select `Pure Gamma` or `Pure LCS` 
> 
> -# *The PP filter that you install later will be specifically made for 1 of these 2 versions; please ensure you double-check this, as otherwise the PP filter will not work as intended.*

---

# Step 6
> 1. Open Content Manager
> 2. Click Drive -> Single -> Weather -> Pure
[attachment: 4.png]

---

# 🚨 Warning: 🚨

> Pure does **NOT** natively come with rain. In order to see rain when it's raining on servers, you must use a **preview version of CSP**. This is a paid version that can be downloaded at: <#1322228581186801745>
