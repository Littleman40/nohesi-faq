# Here Are The Minimum System Requirements To Play No Hesi!

---

### OS:
> Windows 10 or Windows 11
### CPU:
> Intel Core 7xxx / AMD Ryzen 2xxx (6 cores or higher)
### RAM:
> 16GB of DDR4/5 RAM or higher (32GB is HIGHLY recommended!)
### GPU:
> Nvidia GeForce GTX 1060 / AMD Radeon RX 580 or higher
### Storage:
> 100GB SSD (50GB for the game and the rest for modded car packs)
