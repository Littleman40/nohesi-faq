# Whitelisting
> Our servers need to add your Steam ID to our whitelist to allow you to use your VIP perks. To do this, you must connect your Steam account to your No Hesi website account.
> 
> **This website account should have the following linked:**
> * Your main Discord account
> * Your main Steam account
> 
> You can link this by heading over to <https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile> -> scrolling down to "Social Links" -> link both of the above.
