# Turn on and adjust Real Mirrors

---

# Are you driving around and feel like your mirrors are not set up correctly? Follow this guide!
[attachment: image.png]

---

# Method 1
## Step 1
> Open Content Manager and go to Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > Smart Mirror > Real Mirrors > Active
[attachment: Turn_on_and_adjust_Real_Mirrors1.png]

---

## Step 2
> Open Content Manager and go to Settings > Assetto Corsa > Controls > Patch > Real Mirrors > Adjust Tilt controls to your preference
[attachment: Turn_on_and_adjust_Real_Mirrors2.png]
