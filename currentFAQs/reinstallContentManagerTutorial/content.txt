# Reinstall Content Manager Tutorial

---

# Here is how to fully reinstall Content Manager

---

## Step 1
> Delete the Content manager folder
> - Press Windows key + R
> - Type in `%localappdata%` and hit enter 
> - Delete the __"AcTools Content Manager"__ folder.
[attachment: Reinstall_Content_Manager_Tutorial_1.png]

---

## Step 2
**Download and Install 7zip. (*do NOT use WinRAR*)**
> 1. Open https://www.7-zip.org/
> 2. Click "Download"
> 3. Double-click the downloaded exe file
> 4. Follow the prompts to complete the install
[attachment: 1.png]

---

## Step 3
**Download Content Manager**
> Go to **https://acstuff.club/app/**, and click `download directly`
[attachment: DownloadDirectly.png]

---

## Step 4
**Extract Content Manager**
> 1. Go to your downloads folder, and find the `content-manager.zip` folder.
> 2. Right-click -> show more options -> 7zip -> open archive
> 3. Drag and drop `Content Manager.exe` into your desktop
[attachment: 2.png]

---

## Step 5
> Run Content Manager.exe 
> - Select root folder → <#1322235675239645184> 
> - Install all plugins and press OK
[attachment: CMSetup.png]

---

## Step 6
> Close Content Manager, and go back to <#985372285668036639> or <#1524434443434197063> to finish the setup.
