# <:warn:1124835250816692296> Warning <:warn:1124835250816692296> 
> **This FAQ will only fix:**
> * Your chat saying it's disabled
> * A popup saying your car is disabled
> 
> **This will NOT fix:**
> * Your points UI saving the car is vaulted
> * A popup saying: "points disabled for this vehicle"
> -# ^ If you are experiencing these 2, please read: <#1431142566589370548>

---

# The Fix:
> To fix both of these issues, you must **link both your Steam & Discord to your No Hesi website account** under the [**edit profile**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile>) tab.
> 
> **Note:**
> This can take 5-10 minutes to apply, and we suggest restarting your PC during this time.
