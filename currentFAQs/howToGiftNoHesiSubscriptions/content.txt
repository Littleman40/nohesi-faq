How To Gift No Hesi Subscriptions

---

# Feeling generous and want to pay for your friend's VIP subscription? Follow this guide!

---


# Step 1
> Both you and the person you want to gift must have [**No Hesi Website Accounts**](<https://nohesi.gg/signup>)

---

# Step 2
**You must friend the person you wish to gift the subscription to.** 
> To add a friend:
> 1. On the [**home**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/home>) page, under `your friends` search for your friends website username
> 2. Click `Add`
> 3. Ensure your friend accepts this request (*under notifications next to your profile in the navigation bar*)
[attachment: 1.png]


---

# Step 3
> 1. Head over to the [**subscriptions**](<https://nohesi.gg/subscriptions>) tab.
> 2. Click on `Send as a Gift` 
> 3. Select the friend through the dropdown menu.
> 3. Click `Go To Checkout` and follow the prompts to pay.
[attachment: 2.png]


---

# Step 4
**Your friend must redeem the gift:**
> The person who got the gift should head to the [**billing**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/billing>) tab, and click `redeem`
