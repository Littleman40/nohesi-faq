# How to save replays

---

# Want to save your replays with good quality and good length? Follow this guide!

---

## How to save full-length replays:
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Content Manager -> Replays
> 2. Set "Quality" to: `high` or `ultra`
> 2. Change "Maximum Size" to: `8000` 
> -# (click the number to set this, as the slider will not go as high)
> 3. Ensure "Autosave replays" is checked
> 4. Set "Limit for races", "Limit for qualification", and "Limit for other sessions", all to 20.
[attachment: SaveReplay6.png]

---

## What to do after completing your run
> After you finish your run, leave the game.
> 
> A pop-up will appear in Content Manager showing the leaderboard positions and it will have a button that says replay. Hit the arrow next to it and press keep replay.
