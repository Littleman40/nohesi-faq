# Getting #0 place every time you join the server and/or not able to set a new score? Follow this guide!

---

# Prerequisite
> *As per https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1533243188746387496, each run can end by losing 2 lives or the 3-minute timer running out. If by the end of that, you have gotten more points than your previous PB, then your new score will override it, and that will now be your PB.*
> 
> If the above does not happen and your PB is not overridden, then please complete this FAQ.
> 
> **Note**:
> Certain car's and SRP Rush Hour have been vaulted from the leaderboard, as per <#1431142566589370548>. You will **not** be able to set a PB with these.
> 
> If you are receiving a message stating: `you are playing offline`, please complete: <#1512071931736621147>
> 
> If you are receiving a message stating: `car disabled`, please complete: <#1524717666177384468>
> 
> If the point counter is there, but is not counting up/ stuck at 0, please downgrade to a lower CSP 3.0 version, as 520 or above does not work.

---

# Method 1:
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> About & Updates
> 2. Install **0.2.11 CSP**
[attachment: 1.mp4]

---

# Method 2:

> Make sure your computer clock settings are **correctly set, and synced.**

> Right-click the system clock in your **System Tray** at the bottom-right corner of the screen and click  **Adjust date/time** *(You can also go to Settings > Time & Language > Date & time.)*
### Ensure both `set timezone automatically` and `set time automatically` are ticked -> then press `sync now`.
[attachment: 2.mp4]

---

-# **If any of these options are greyed out, please watch <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPq2R-vxCTw> first.**

### A full explanation + other debugging steps can be found on the article: <https://www.makeuseof.com/tag/3-reasons-windows-computer-loses-time-date/>

---

# Method 3
> Ensure your **antivirus **/ **firewall **/ **ISP** are not blocking the connection to the server.
> 
> 1. **Antiviruses** -> Disable these or allow exceptions for Assetto Corsa and Content Manager
> 2. **Firewalls **-> Allow exceptions for Assetto Corsa and Content Manager
> 3. **ISPs** -> If this applies to you, most common for **Russian Users**, you will be required to use a **VPN** or **Proxy **to bypass this.
