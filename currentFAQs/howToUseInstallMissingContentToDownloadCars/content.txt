# How to use "Install Missing Content" to download cars

---

# Wondering how to install No Hesi cars? Follow this guide!
[attachment: image.png]

---

# Prerequisites:
> ## Step 1:
> Ensure you have done: https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1322223328735399989 
> 
> ## Step 2:
> 1. Open Content Manager -> Settings -> Content Manager -> Content -> Downloading Content -> Temporary Files Location
> 
> 2. Ensure this is set to a folder on your computer ***that is not full***. Best practice is to create a new folder for this.
[attachment: How_to_properly_setup_working_Install_Missing_Content_button.png]

---

## Step 1
> Press Install missing content
[attachment: How_to_use_Install_Missing_Content_to_download_cars.png]

---

## Step 2. 
> At the top right, open the ≡ menu, wait for the car(s) or track(s) to download and then press Install.
[attachment: How_to_use_Install_Missing_Content_to_download_cars2.png]

---

# Step 3:
> Restart Content Manager or restart your computer.
