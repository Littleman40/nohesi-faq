# What Are Vaulted Cars?
> To keep the leaderboard fair, No Hesi will vault any overpowered cars. This stops them from counting toward the leaderboard and ensures the leaderboard is filled with a variety of cars, not a singular OP car.

# Warning UI / Pop-Up UI
> When you are playing with these cars, you will see the message `points won't save to LB for this vehicle` under the points UI.
