# Series 6.7 Update:
> With the new series, **joining a crew happens automatically**, when you get into a close proximity to another player. We removed the need to create an explicit crew group.
> 
> *In order to avoid griefing, we highly suggest you use Passive Mode: <#1383614143202070548> *
> 
> **<:warn:1124835250816692296> Note <:warn:1124835250816692296> **:
> If you drive on the shoulder for 3 seconds straight, your proximity will stop. You will need to then drive completely off the shoulder again for another 3 seconds, in order to gain these proximity bonus again.
