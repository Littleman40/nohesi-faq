# Missing VIP Roles

---

# Bought VIP, but you didn't get any Discord roles? Follow this guide!

---

# Method 1: 
> Log into [nohesi.gg](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile>) and click your **profile picture** -> **edit profile**
> 
> Ensure the **correct discord account** / **steam account** is linked.
[attachment: MissingVIP1.png]

---

# Method 2:
> **unlink + relink** your Discord account.

---

# Method 3:
> 1. On the website -> click your **profile picture** -> **billing** -> **cancel**. From here, follow the cancel prompts. 
> 2. Reactivate the subscription with the prompt on the billing page.
