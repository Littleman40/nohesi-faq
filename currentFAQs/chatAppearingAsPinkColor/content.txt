# Chat appearing as pink color

---

# Do you have a pink/inverted colored chat? Follow this guide!
[attachment: image.png]

---

## Step 1
> Open Content Manager > Go to Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > About & Updates

## Step 2
> Install CSP Version 0.2.11
> If already on CSP version 0.2.11, click `Reinstall Current Version`
[attachment: image_2.png]

---

## Step 3
> Restart Content Manager
