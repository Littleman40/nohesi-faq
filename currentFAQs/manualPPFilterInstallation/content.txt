# <:warn:1124835250816692296> Warning <:warn:1124835250816692296> 
> Most PP filters come with their own installation guides. It is **imperative that you follow their instructions** and not these. **Certain PP filters may require certain CSP/ Pure versions, and they may have their own install guide**, eg through bat files, etc. This guide is mercifully only a simple guide on how most, but not all, basic PP filters are installed. 
> 
> **We do not guarantee this will work**, and we always suggest finding the creator's install guide for the most accurate guide.

---

## Step 1
> **Download & Install 7zip:**
> 1. Open https://www.7-zip.org/
> 2. Click "Download"
> 3. Double-click the downloaded exe file
> 4. Follow the prompts to complete the install
[attachment: 1.png]

---

## Step 2
> **Install the PP filter:**
> 1. Find the relevant ZIP file in your downloads folder → right-click → `show more options` → `7zip` → `open archive`
> 2. You will usually find a few folders inside. You need to drag & drop these into your root folder, <#1322235675239645184>. (**do NOT drag & drop into Content Manager**)
> 
> **Note:**
> If the folder only contains a folder called: `assettocorsa`, please open it and drag the contents of that folder into your root folder.

---

# Step 3
> **Set up the PP filter:**
> 1. Open Content Manager → Settings → Assetto Corsa → Video → Post Processing
> 2. Select the PP filter from the drop down menu
> 3. Head to Content Manager → Settings → Custom Shaders Patch → WeatherFX
> 4. Under `Weather Style` select the correct weather style for the PP filter you installed.
