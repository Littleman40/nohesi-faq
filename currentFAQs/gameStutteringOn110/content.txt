# Are you experiencing game freezes and stuttering while playing 110? Follow this guide!

---

## Method 1
> Go to Content Manager > Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > General Patch Settings > CPU Optimizations
> 
> Set Chunk Optimization to **`basic`** or **`disabled`**
