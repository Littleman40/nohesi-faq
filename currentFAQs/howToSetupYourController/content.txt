# How to setup your Controller

---

# Have a controller but it does not work in-game? Follow this guide!
[attachment: SetupcontrollerLOGO.png]

---

# Step 1
> 1. Open Steam
> 2. Click `Steam` in the top left -> Settings -> Controller
> 3. Press `Show Advanced Settings`
> 4. Depending on which controller you have, enable its Steam Input:
>   * **Xbox**: Enable `Enable Steam Input for Xbox controllers`
>   * **PlayStation**: Set `PlayStation Controller Support` to enabled
>   * **Switch Pro**: Enable `Enable Steam Input for Switch Pro controllers`
>   * **Any other brand**: Enable `Enable Steam Input for generic controllers`
[attachment: 1.png]

---

[attachment: 2.png]

---

# Step 2
> Make sure Steam Input is enabled:
> 1. Open Steam -> Library -> Assetto Corsa -> Controller Icon
> 2. You then want to click the A button/next until you see Enable Steam Input.
[attachment: 3.png]

---

[attachment: 4.png]

---

# Step 3
> You **MUST** now switch to Steam starter. You can do it properly by following: <#1486631384402624513>

---

# Step 4
> Open Content Manager, by launching the game from Steam, and go to Settings > Assetto Corsa > Controls > Main
> 
> 1. Set input method: Gamepad
> 2. Select Gamepad ID: 1
> 3. Rebind all the `(placeholder)` binds.
> 
> **IF YOU ARE** using a PlayStation 4 or 5 gamepad, tick the box!
[attachment: How_to_setup_your_Controller1.png]

---

-# Any binds that say Preset can ***NOT*** be changed.
-# If you want settings, check out https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1189409395226984518
-# For assists and presets head to **gamepad fx** in CSP settings
