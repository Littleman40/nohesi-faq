# Error 0xc000007b

---

# Here is how to fix *Error 0xc000007b - slimdx.dll could not be found*
[attachment: Error_0xc000007bLOGO.png]

---

# Step 1
**Verify your game files**:
> 1. Open Steam → Library → Assetto Corsa
> 2. Right-click AC → Properties → Installed Files
> 3. Click `Verify integrity of game files`
> 4. Wait for it to finish before moving on.
[attachment: Verify_game_files_for_Assetto_Corsa1.png]

---

[attachment: Verify_game_files_for_Assetto_Corsa2.png]

---

# Step 2
> Ensure you have started the game, Assetto Corsa, directly from your Steam library before.
> 
> Once you get to the main menu, close the game.
[attachment: LaunchBaseGame.png]

---

# Step 3
**Download & Install all the dependencies:**
> - [**DirectX End-User Runtime Web**](<https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/download/details.aspx?id=35>)
> - [**DirectX End-User Runtimes June 2010**](<https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/download/details.aspx?id=8109>)
> - [**.Net Framework 4.8.1**](<https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/download/dotnet-framework/net481>)
> - [**C++ Runtime Packages**](<https://www.techpowerup.com/download/visual-c-redistributable-runtime-package-all-in-one/>)
> 
> **Note:**
> The C++ link will download a ZIP file. Please extract this and run the `install_all.bat` file, **in administrator mode**

---

# Step 4
> Restart your PC and play
