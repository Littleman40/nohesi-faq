# How to install Shutoko (SRP)
[attachment: preview.png]

---

# Step 1
**Download and Install 7zip. (*do NOT use WinRAR*)**
> 1. Open https://www.7-zip.org/
> 2. Click "Download"
> 3. Double-click the downloaded exe file
> 4. Follow the prompts to complete the install
[attachment: 7zip.png]

---

# Step 2: 
**Download the Shuto Revival Project Track**
> 1. Open [**0.9.3 Shutoko Link**](<https://shutokorevivalproject.com/#download>)
> 2. Click "Download" next to "Stable Release 0.9.3"
[attachment: Shuto_is_missing.png]

---

# Step 3:
**Install the Shuto Revival Project Track**
> 1. Right Click -> (*Show More Options*) -> 7zip -> Open Archive
> 2. Drag & drop "content" into your root folder: <#1322235675239645184>
[attachment: installGuideImg.png]

---

# Step 4:
> **Restart your PC**

---

## 🚨  If you have had 0.9.1 installed previously, you are required to delete it fully from your track folder first 🚨
> 1. Go to `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\assettocorsa\content\tracks`
> 2. Right-click on shuto_revival_project_beta -> Click delete.
