CSP Installation Is Damaged ( Version Mismatch )

---

# Are you receiving this CSP mismatch error? Follow this guide!
[attachment: 1.png]

---

# Step 1
**Download and Install 7zip. (*do NOT use WinRAR*)**
> 1. Open https://www.7-zip.org/
> 2. Click "Download"
> 3. Double-click the downloaded exe file
> 4. Follow the prompts to complete the install
[attachment: 2.png]

---

# Step 2
> 1. Open Content Manager
> 2. Click Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> About & Updates
> 3. Click the `Delete patch`
[attachment: 3.png]

---

# Step 3
> **Download Custom Shaders Patch, without using Content Manager, as per the following:**
> 
> ### CSP Preview:
> If you are using a **CSP Preview Version**, please download the latest version from the [**paid Patreon**](<https://www.patreon.com/c/x4fab/home>)
> 
> ### Non-Preview CSP:
> If you are not paying for preview versions, please download 0.2.11 CSP from the [**ACStuff Website**](<https://acstuff.club/patch/?get=0.2.11>)

---

# Step 4
> **Manually install CSP without using Content Manager, as per the following:**
> 
> 1. Locate the downloaded ZIP from the preview step.
> 2. Right-click -> *show more options* -> 7zip -> open archive
> 3. Drag and drop both `extension` and `dwrite.dll` into your AC Root Folder: <#1322235675239645184>
> 
> <:warn:1124835250816692296> **Warning **<:warn:1124835250816692296> 
> Do not drag & drop them into Content Manager, as this can cause this issue.
[attachment: 4.png]

---

# Step 5
> **Restart your PC**
