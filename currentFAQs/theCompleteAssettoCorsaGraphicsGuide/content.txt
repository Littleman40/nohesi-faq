# The complete Assetto Corsa graphics guide

---

## Want to get a good looking game? Heres how:

---

# Step 1 - Weather System
> There are 2 options for weather systems:
> 1. **Pure**: <#1322227974371545159>
> 2. **Sol**: <#1511252401343041697>
> 
> Pure is the paid version (only 1 dollar) and is actively maintained by the creator. **This is the version that we suggest you get, and this is the version that most post-processing filters require.**
> 
> Sol is the older, free alternative, which is no longer maintained. **Warning:** Sol does not work with newer CSP versions, and therefore is not recommended.
> 
> **Install Guide:**
> Please follow the install guides in the FAQ's linked above. You must install this manually; **do NOT drag & drop into Content Manager! ****

---

# Step 2 - Rain
> To add rain to your weather system, **you must have** **[Pure](<https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1322227974371545159>)** installed. You can't use rain while using sol.
> 
> To add Rain, you will need a preview version of CSP. This is paid and costs 1 dollar. 
> 
> **Install Guide:**
> This can be downloaded/ paid for through: <#1322228581186801745>

---

# Step 3 - PP Filter
> To further customise how your game looks, install a post-processing filter. 
> 
> The PP filters you install will be dependent on whether you installed Pure or Sol. 
> * Pure PP Filters: <#1358606823388872775>
> * Sol PP Filters: <#1358607528845508708>
> 
> **Warning:**
> Some PP filters will require certain CSP versions/ pure versions. Please read each PP filter's install guide carefully!
> 
> **Install Guide:**
> You must always install PP filters manually. All PP filters should come with their own install guide that explains how to install it. Here is a general install guide; however, it may not apply to your selected filter: <#1358606555574173786>

# Step 4 - CSP Settings 
> Most PP filters will come with recommended CSP/ video setting presets that you can apply directly. However, if not, you can find presets in share settings: <#1189409395226984518>
> 
> ⚠️ **Never pay for CSP settings! Most sellers have no real expertise and are just reselling basic tweaks that you can find for free or easily adjust yourself.**
