# Experiencing cutting / glitchy audio? Follow this Guide!

---

## Traffic 2.0 Prerequisite:
**If you are on traffic 2.0 servers and have no sound, please follow:**
> 1. While in-game, move your mouse to the right-hand side, and a bar will pop out
> 2. Search up "audio", and enable the app that shows up
> 3. Move the master slider away from 0% to your desired volume level

---

## Method 1
> Open Content Manager > Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > General Patch Settings > Scroll to *Audio* > Uncheck "Silence Malfunctioning FMOD channels”
[attachment: CuttingGlitchAudio1.png]

---

# Method 2
> 1. While in game, move your mouse all the way to the right.
> 2. Search up `audio` in the pop-out menu
> 3. Lower `master` and `engine` audio until the sounds stop clipping
[attachment: CuttingGlitchAudio2.png]
