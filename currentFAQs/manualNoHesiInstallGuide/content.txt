# Want to install No Hesi manually? Follow this guide!

---

# Prerequisites
> **We have a No Hesi installer, which handles the full setup in 8 simple steps, including downloads of required content! This can be found at** <#985372285668036639>
> 
> The FAQ below is for those wishing to complete a manual install.

---

# Step 1:
> Ensure you meet PC requirements: <#1511242216679542814>

---

# Step 2:
**Buy Assetto Corsa**
> Click [**here**](<https://store.steampowered.com/app/244210/Assetto_Corsa/>) to purchase it from Steam. (***DLC's are NOT required***)
> 
> <:warn:1124835250816692296> **Once downloaded, ensure you run it at least once from Steam before continuing with this guide** <:warn:1124835250816692296>

---

# Step 3:
**Download Content Manager**
> 1. Head to https://acstuff.ru/app 
> 2. Scroll down and click `download directly`
> 
> *This will be the launcher for Assetto Corsa*
[attachment: 1.mp4]

---

> Navigate to the download and right click, then extract the files.
[attachment: 2.mp4]

---

> Open the folder you extracted the files to and launch Content Manager.
[attachment: 3.mp4]

---

# Step 4:
**Setup Content Manager**
> You will be need to locate your AC root folder, explained in the next message.
[attachment: 4.mp4]

---

> Go into your Steam Library, Right-click AC and then Manage > Browse Local Files.
[attachment: 5.mp4]

---

> A folder will open, and you can see the path to the root folder. Copy this path.
[attachment: 6.mp4]

---

> Back in Content Manager, double-click the preset root folder and paste your own one.
[attachment: 7.mp4]

---

> Now enter your Steam ID, if it's not there automatically. To find your steam ID: <#1322217411876753449>
[attachment: 8.mp4]

---

> Install the first two plugins and click OK
[attachment: 9.mp4]

---

> Click Settings, then Custom Shader Patch, and click `Install Patch`. Make sure you're always on the latest version.
[attachment: CSPInstall.png]

# Step 7
**Create a No Hesi account**
> 1. Head to [**nohesi.gg**](<https://nohesi.gg/signup>) and create an account
> 2. Link both Discord and Steam under the [**edit profile**](<https://nohesi.gg/manage/profile>) tab

---

# Step 8
**Join a server**
> You can find all our servers under: <https://nohesi.gg/servers>
> 
> Once you join, use `install missing content` to download the required cars, following the guide: <#1322223184367587462>
