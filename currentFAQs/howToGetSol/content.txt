How To Get Sol

---

# Here is how to install **Sol**, a free alternative to Pure

[attachment: SolBackground.png]

---

# 🚨  Warning 🚨

---

## Sol will NOT work with CSP versions listed below:
* **0.2.10**
* **0.2.11**
* **0.2.11 Preview**
* **0.2.12 Preview**

If you wish to use Sol, please revert back to earlier CSP versions or use 0.3.xxx CSP

---

## Step 1
> Download Sol from [**Overtake.GG**](<https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/sol.24914/>)
> -# Overtake.GG requires an account;
> -# Please create an account. **Scroll all the way down to "Join Basic press here" > press on the *Here* and enter your Email, Username and Password**

[attachment: SolClickToDownload.png]

---

## Step 2
> Right click on Sol 2.2.x and click Extract Here

[attachment: SolExtract.png]

---

## Step 3
> Open the new Sol 2.2.x folder and drag the Apps, Content, Extension and System files into the AC root folder.
> -# **Always install Sol by manually copying its files to the Assetto Corsa root folder!*

[attachment: SolRootFolderInstall.png]


--- 

## Step 4
> Open Content Manager > Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > WeatherFX > Weather Style > Sol

---

## Note:
> Never install both Pure and Sol at the same time. *This will cause major issues, which can include FPS drops*

---

# How To Uninstall Sol

---

# Step 1:
> Inside the sol download folder, which you can download from above, locate the **soluninstall.bat** file

---

# Step 2:
> Move this **soluninstall.bat file into your root folder**, which can be found as per the following: https://discord.com/channels/964645662866173972/1322235675239645184

---

# Step 3:
> Double-click the bat file, and follow the prompts to uninstall Sol.
> 
> **Make sure to restart your PC afterwards.**
