# Broken Neck FX ?

---

# Is the driver's head not moving even with NeckFX on? Follow this guide!
[attachment: BrokenNeckFXLOGO.png]

---

## Method 1
> Open Content Manager and go to **Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > NeckFX**
> - Extension **(4)**
>       - **Enable "Active"**
> 
> - Replace Original CSP implementation with a custom script **(5)**
>       - **Disable "Active"**
[attachment: 1.png]

---

## Method 2
> If your Neck FX is still not working, open Content Manager, and go to **Settings > Assetto Corsa > Video**
> - Rendering mode
>      - Select **"Single screen"**
[attachment: 2.png]
