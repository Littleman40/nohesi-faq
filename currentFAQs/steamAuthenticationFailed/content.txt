# Steam Authentication Failed?

---

# Here is how to fix " Steam Authentication Failed "
[attachment: Steam_Authentication_FailedLOGO.png]

---

# Steam servers are down for maintenance every Tuesday
> ### Please be patient as they come back online after 10 minutes to 1 hour.\

---

## If it isn't a Tuesday, and Steam isn't having maintenance/ issues, please follow the rest:

---

# Method 1:
**Ensure you own a legit copy of Assetto Corsa, and you are NOT using Steam family sharing:**
> If you ever pirated AC or any DLC's, you must reinstall everything following: <#1437114487051255858>
> 
> If you are a part of family sharing, please remove yourself from it, and purchase the game on your own account.

---

# Method 2:
**Ensure you have whitelisted following:**
<#1541308743340392538>

> *Whitelisting may take 5-10 minutes to apply, and we suggest you restart your pc in that time.*

---

# Method 3:
> 1. Open Content Manager
> 2. Click Settings -> Custom Shaders Patch -> About & Updates
> 3. Install **0.2.11 CSP**
> 4. Restart your PC

---

# Method 4:
> 1. Ensure you are logged into steam
> 2. Ensure that you are not in *offline mode* on steam
> 3. Ensure your game files are up to date: <#1322222319518748692>
[attachment: 1.png]

---

# Method 5:
> 1. Ensure you have set a **Name **and **Online Name**, **that isnt: `player`** and that consists of **only letters/numbers, NO SYMBOLS**
> 2. **Disable Online Name**
[attachment: 2.png]

---

# Method 6:
**Ensure the correct SteamID is linked in Content Manager:**
> 1. Open Content Manager
> 2. Click Settings -> Content Manager -> General
> 3. Ensure this SteamID matches your SteamID: <#1322217411876753449>
[attachment: 3.png]

---

# Method 7:
> 1. Close **Steam** and **Content Manager** with **Task Manager** by right clicking the programs and select **END TASK**.
> 2. Launch **Steam** first, followed by **Content Manager** and try to join a server again.
[attachment: 4.png]

---

# Method 8:
> Switch to Steam Starter following: <#1486631384402624513>

---
