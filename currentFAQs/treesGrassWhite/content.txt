# Trees / Grass White

# Are your trees and grass white and you do not like it? Follow this guide!
[attachment: White_Trees_LOGO.png]

---

## Method 1
> Open Content Manager and go to Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > Weather FX
> Next, Uncheck the box labeled "Use Seasonal Adjustments"
[attachment: Trees__Grass_White1.png]

---

## Method 2
> In the event it is not in Weather FX, the setting may be in 'Track Adjustments'
