# Remove SafeMode Resolution Correction

---

# Here is how to fix “Running in SafeMode Resolution Correction”
[attachment: RemoveSafemodeLOGO.png]

---

# Step 1:
**Find your monitors resolution + hz**
> Open window settings, by pressing `windows + I` -> Click `System` -> `Display` -> `Advanced Display`
> 
> If you have multiple monitors, select the correct display from the dropdown.
> 
> Take note of the resolution, usually it will be `1920x1090` or `2560x1440`
> 
> Also take note of the HZ, which is next to the resolution.

---

# Step 2:
**Set the correct resolution + Hz inside CM**
> Open Content Manager and go to Settings > Assetto Corsa > Video 
> 
> From the dropdown, match the correct resolution + Hz to what you found in step 1.
[attachment: Remove_SafeMode_Resolution_Correction.png]
